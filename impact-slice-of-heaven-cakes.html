Every cake order in one place, answered the moment it arrives.

Slice of Heaven Vintage Cakes makes custom vintage cakes for weddings, birthdays and other celebrations in Tampa, run by one baker. Every order and quote request landed in an inbox, where each was read, priced and answered by hand.

Now every request from the site arrives in one place. The customer gets a confirmation the moment they hit submit, an order puts itself on the calendar, and the request moves along a pipeline from new to quoted to confirmed, so nothing is answered twice or missed.

Quoting happens there too. She opens a request, builds the price from her own menu of tiers, flavors, fillings and extras, and sends it as a finished document in a few clicks.

Related: how to audit a workflow before automating it and Zapier or an AI agent: which one a job needs.

What the owner stopped doing.

It removed the typing around her judgment.

Retyping the order

Date, servings, flavors, fillings, allergies and inspiration arrive structured and stay attached to the request through to the quote.

Answering the same first email

The confirmation goes out the instant a request arrives, so nobody wonders whether the form worked while she is in the kitchen.

Why this is worth doing for a business of one.

The parts were there. The wiring between them was missing.

The tools were already paid for

A form, spreadsheet, inbox and calendar she already had. Connecting them added no monthly cost and ended the hand-carrying between them.

It holds up in wedding season

One view of every live request, with its price and status, makes a heavy week feel like a quiet one.

The same wiring works for anything booked by request.

If you take orders through a form and answer them by hand, we connect what you already use into one place to work from.
