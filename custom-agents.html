What we automate.

If someone on your team opens it, reads it, checks it against something, and types it somewhere else, it is a good candidate.

Orders

Customer purchase orders that arrive by email become sales orders at your prices. Anything priced below your list, or an item you do not carry, is flagged before it ships.

Bills

Supplier bills are matched to the right job and purchase order and sorted by cost code, ready for QuickBooks. A bill with no PO, or one you have already seen, waits for you.

Paperwork you chase

W-9s and insurance certificates are requested from every sub or vendor, checked against your requirements, and asked for again before they expire.

Intake and requests

Web forms, emails, and requests become organized records with the next step attached, whether that is a new client, a cake order, or an event request.

Documents and reports

Receipts become expense reports, forms become finished PDFs, and the weekly summary gets drafted from the systems the numbers already live in.

Custom agents

When nothing off the shelf fits, we build an agent that reads what comes in, decides what to do next, and works across your tools through a connector, with a person approving anything that matters. See one we built.

Nobody on your team or theirs has to change how they work.

Your customers and vendors keep emailing the way they do now. The system reads each document, checks it against your own records, and handles the ones that add up. The ones that do not land in front of a person, with a note saying what looks wrong, to approve or send back.

Not everything needs AI. If the steps never change and the information arrives clean, a plain automation in Zapier or Make is cheaper and easier to fix, and we will say so. AI earns its place when someone has to read a messy document, make a judgment call, or pull information from several places first. Zapier vs AI agents walks through that choice, and what an AI agent is covers the basics.

Questions we get before an automation.

Do we need an audit or training first?

No. Automation stands on its own. If you already know which job you want off your team's plate, we can start there.

Do we have to change the tools we use?

No. We build around what you already run, such as QuickBooks, your inbox, and your CRM. Your team, customers, and vendors keep working the way they do now.

What happens when it gets something wrong?

Anything that does not match your records waits for a person to approve or send back. Nothing is sent or paid without your say.

Who owns the system after launch?

Your team. Every engagement ends with the prompts, system instructions, documentation, and operating notes handed over, so the people who own the workflow can keep changing it without us.

Send us twenty recent examples of the paperwork.

Bills, orders, forms, or whatever your team retypes. We will show you how each one would have been handled, and what we would have caught, before you commit to anything.
