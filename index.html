Questions owners ask us first.

We already pay for ChatGPT and almost nobody uses it. Why would this be different?

Because we train people on their own work, not on features. Each person leaves with something that already does part of their job, saved where the whole team can use it.

Do we have to buy all three services?

No. Each service stands on its own. Plenty of teams only want training, or only want one job automated. If one leads to another, it is quoted separately.

I do not have an IT person. Who keeps this running?

We do, for as long as you want. Everything runs on the tools you already have, and you get the notes to take it over whenever you like.

Is this going to replace my staff?

No. It takes the retyping and chasing off their plate so they can do the work you hired them for. Most owners use the time to grow without hiring.

Is our data safe?

Your documents are stored encrypted, seen only by your team and ours, and never used to train AI. Nothing gets sent or approved without your say.

We are not a tech company. Are we too early for this?

Small, non-technical teams often get the most out of it, because every hour back is a bigger share of the week. Nobody needs a technical background.

AI tools are easy to buy. The hard part is making them reliable inside work your team already depends on.
