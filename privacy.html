Privacy Policy

Uncreated LLC d/b/a altr. Version 1.2. Effective October 11, 2026.

This policy covers altrwork.com, the calls you book with us (including calls with Kara, our AI intake assistant), our free workshops, and the email we send you. It is short because we collect little. If something here is not true of us, we left it out on purpose rather than pad the page.

Uncreated LLC, a Florida limited liability company, d/b/a altr, of Tampa / St. Petersburg, Florida. Questions: privacy@altrwork.com.

What we collect

What you type into a form. Our website forms ask for a work email address, and sometimes a name, a company, and a short description of what you are trying to do. That is all we ask for and all we want. Do not put confidential or sensitive information into a website form.

What you give us when you book a call. When you book through our booking page, we receive your name, email address, phone number, company, any notes you add, the time you picked, and whether you ticked the box asking Kara to call you (with the time you ticked it).

Calls with Kara. Kara is an AI voice assistant, not a person, and she says so. Calls with Kara are recorded and transcribed. At the start of every call she asks whether you agree to the recording; if you say no, she ends the call and Alex or Jarred follows up by email instead. From a call we keep the recording, the transcript, and a written summary for the founders. Kara only calls you if you booked a call and ticked the box asking her to, and only between 8am and 8pm in your time zone. If you call Kara's number yourself, she asks about recording in the same way.

Research before a call. Before a call, we search the public web for your company so the call can be short and useful. We do not buy data about you or use data brokers.

Meetings with us. When you meet with Alex or Jarred, we take notes, and we may use an AI note-taking tool to help. Those notes are kept with our client records.

Workshop sign-ups. When you register for one of our free workshops, the registration platform shares your name, email address, and the answers you give on the registration form with us.

What your browser sends. Like every website, our host records IP address, browser and device type, referring page, and the pages you visited. We use this to keep the site up and to understand which pages are worth writing more of.

Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages get read. It sets cookies and collects usage data. We strip names, email addresses, and phone numbers out of page addresses before analytics sees them. We do not use analytics to build advertising profiles, we do not run ad retargeting pixels, and we do not sell anything to data brokers.

Nothing else. We do not collect payment information on this website. When you pay us, you pay through Stripe on Stripe's own pages, and we never see or store your card or bank numbers.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor gave us information, email privacy@altrwork.com and we will delete it.

Why we use it

  • To send you the thing you asked for, such as a field guide.
  • To hold the call you booked, prepare for it, and follow up on it.
  • To reply to you, and to do the work you hire us for.
  • To send occasional practical updates about our work, if you asked for those.
  • To keep the site running, secure, and worth reading.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have never done either and have no plan to.

How we use AI with your information

We are an AI company and we use AI in our own work. In particular, an AI model writes the pre-call research and the summary of your call with Kara, and our internal assistants help us draft follow-ups, proposals, and records. A person at altr reads what they produce, and nothing is sent to you without a person approving it. We do not use AI to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

The AI services we use are run by the vendors listed below, under their business terms. How each vendor handles and retains data is governed by its own terms and settings, which can change, so we do not make promises on their behalf.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, to protect our rights or someone's safety, or to a buyer as part of a sale of the business, in which case this policy follows the data.

Calls and texts

  • Kara only places a call you booked and asked for. She does not cold call, and we do not use her for marketing calls to people who have not asked.
  • If you tell Kara, or us, that you do not want to be called again, we record that and she will not call your number again.
  • We do not send marketing text messages.

Email

If you asked for updates, we send them. Every email has a working unsubscribe link, and we honor it promptly. We do not sell or rent our list, we do not send on behalf of third parties, and we do not use misleading subject lines. Our mailing address is in the footer of every message, as CAN-SPAM requires.

Unsubscribing stops the updates. It does not stop replies to a conversation you started, or messages about work we are doing for you.

How long we keep it

  • Form submissions, bookings, call recordings, transcripts, call summaries, and email list entries: until you ask us to remove them, or until three years after your last interaction with us, whichever comes first.
  • A request not to be called: for as long as we run Kara, so that we keep honoring it.
  • Analytics data: on Google's retention schedule, currently 14 months.
  • Client records, invoices, and contracts: seven years, because tax and professional obligations require it.

How we protect it

We use access controls on our accounts and devices, keep credentials out of source control and out of client documents, restrict who on our side can see what, and keep a record of who accessed our internal systems. No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise.

If we determine that a breach affected personal information about Florida residents, we will notify affected individuals within 30 days of that determination, and the Florida Department of Legal Affairs where the Florida Information Protection Act, section 501.171, Florida Statutes, requires it.

Your choices

You can, at any time, and regardless of where you live:

  • Ask what we have about you. Email privacy@altrwork.com.
  • Ask us to correct it.
  • Ask us to delete it, including a call recording or transcript. We will, except where we have to keep it for tax or legal reasons, and we will tell you if that applies.
  • Say no to a recording. Tell Kara at the start of the call, and a person will follow up by email instead.
  • Stop calls. Tell Kara or email us, and we will not call your number again.
  • Unsubscribe from email, using the link in any message.
  • Turn off analytics with Google's opt-out add-on, or with any browser setting or extension that blocks it. The site works fine without it.

We answer these within 30 days. We do not charge for it and we do not treat you differently for asking.

Comprehensive state privacy laws such as the Florida Digital Bill of Rights and the California Consumer Privacy Act apply to companies far larger than we are, so they do not currently govern us. We offer the rights above anyway, because the alternative is a page explaining why we would not.

People outside the United States

We operate in the United States and our vendors store data here. If you contact us from elsewhere, your information will be processed in the United States, under United States law. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom and want your data removed, email privacy@altrwork.com and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will change the version and date at the top. Where a change is significant and we have your email, we will tell you.
