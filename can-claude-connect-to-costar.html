Can Claude connect to CoStar?

Jarred RobidouxAugust 30, 2026Updated September 28, 2026

"Can you just hook Claude up to CoStar?" I hear some version of that in almost every CRE conversation. The answer is no. The useful part is why, and what your team should automate instead.

Short answer

No. As of September 2026 Claude cannot connect to CoStar: there is no CoStar connector, and CoStar's public Terms of Use, last revised July 6, 2026, bar sharing passcodes with AI agents, scraping, and using CoStar data as input to generative AI or retrieval-augmented generation. The same limits apply to ChatGPT. Automate the work around CoStar with your own systems and public records.

Why is there no CoStar connector in Claude?

Open Customize > Connectors and search for CoStar. It is not there. That is not a gap Anthropic forgot to fill: CoStar's terms are written to keep its licensed data out of outside AI tools, so a connector would contradict them.

The request usually comes right after a principal buys Claude seats. The team already pays for CoStar and wants comps and market context without copying screens into a chat window. That is a reasonable want. It is just not a CoStar problem.

What do CoStar's terms actually prohibit?

CoStar's public Terms of Use, last revised July 6, 2026, rule out the three shortcuts teams ask for. This is not legal advice, and your signed license agreement governs your account.

  1. Sharing the login with an AI agent. Passcodes may not be shared with third-party AI services, including AI agents. The terms also let CoStar raise fees for additional users it detects.
  2. Scraping. Scraping, data mining, robots, spiders and extraction tools are prohibited. A "connector" that signs in as a user and reads the screens is exactly this.
  3. Exporting a slice into Claude. You may not expose the licensed product to AI tools outside CoStar, or use data generated from it to train an AI system or as input to generative AI or retrieval-augmented generation.

None of this is specific to Claude. It covers any assistant, agent or retrieval pipeline.

What should a CRE team connect instead?

What the team wantsPath that is allowed
Comparable salesCounty deed and property appraiser records through a custom connector, plus your own closed deals.
Who owns a buildingThe county roll for the owner of record, then the state corporate registry for the people behind the LLC. In Florida that is Sunbiz.
Market data in ClaudeA vendor that licenses it for AI use and ships a connector, such as Yardi Matrix for multifamily.
UnderwritingA Skill built on the OM, your rent roll and your criteria.
A CoStar market reportRead it in CoStar. Write your own conclusions into the memo.

Start with systems the team owns: the CRM, Yardi and the files already on disk. That is the connectors work, and it is allowed because the systems and the data are yours. Run the file work in Cowork, and put recurring research on scheduled tasks against sources you are allowed to automate.

For Florida property, we built a county records connector that answers ownership and comps questions from public record, with each figure naming its source file and date.

Someone still opens CoStar when they need CoStar. Claude does the repeatable work around it. If Claude only works when someone pastes CoStar into the chat, the team does not have a CoStar problem. It has a workflow problem.

Common questions

Is there an official CoStar connector for Claude?

No. As of September 2026 CoStar is not in Claude's connector directory and has not announced a Claude connector. The directory changes often, so check Customize > Connectors before relying on this.

Can I paste a CoStar export into Claude?

CoStar's public Terms of Use say you may not expose the licensed product to AI tools outside CoStar or use data generated from it as input to generative AI or retrieval-augmented generation. Your signed license governs your account, so read it before you do this.

Does this apply to ChatGPT and Copilot too?

Yes. The terms restrict AI tools outside CoStar in general, not Claude in particular. Any assistant, agent or retrieval pipeline given CoStar credentials or CoStar data falls under the same restrictions.

What can Claude use for comps instead of CoStar?

Public records such as county property appraiser and deed data, your own closed deals and rent rolls, and market data from vendors whose license allows AI use. Yardi, for example, publishes a Claude connector for its Yardi Matrix market data.

Working line

Do not ask Claude to log into CoStar. Ask it to run the work you are allowed to automate, on a cadence, with a person who owns the output.
