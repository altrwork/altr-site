Claude in CRE: Connectors

Jarred RobidouxAugust 11, 20264 min read

If “we have Claude review emails” still means copying an email into a chat box, the model is separated from the systems where your team does the work.

The shift

A connector bridges Claude directly to an external app, service, or data source. That turns it from a chatbot waiting for pasted context into a working member of the team.

What is a connector?

Connectors give Claude live access to the applications you use throughout the day. Instead of copying an email, exporting a report, or pasting CRM notes, Claude can work with the source directly—within the permissions you approve.

In Claude, start under Customize → Connectors. You can browse supported connectors or add a custom connector. Hundreds of common applications already have integrations available; custom connectors cover the systems that do not.

Connect the CRM first

Commercial real estate is a people-oriented business. New contacts come from golf courses, networking events, happy hours, tours, and referrals. The CRM is often the easiest place to show immediate value.

Once Claude is connected, a stack of business cards or prospecting notes can become structured contacts without manually retyping every field. The same connection can help prepare follow-ups, summarize relationship history, and keep the pipeline current.

Connect property operations

Yardi is an operational backbone for many CRE teams, holding rent, lease terms, tenant billing, work orders, and financial reporting. Connecting Claude to a property system removes the constant cycle of exporting reports and digging through rows in Excel.

The same pattern applies to RentManager, AppFolio, a deal tracker, or another system with an API: give Claude a controlled door to the live data instead of asking the team to carry the data into every conversation.

When the connector does not exist

Custom connectors are useful when the work depends on a private system or a market source without a ready-made integration.

Imagine evaluating an 80,000-square-foot industrial warehouse in Tampa. Due diligence often starts with comp research across tools such as CoStar, Crexi, or Reonomy. When no public API is available, a custom workflow can pull from county deed records, match ownership through Sunbiz, and bring the resulting property and owner context into Claude.

Most modern software with an API can be connected. Public records and browser-based sources require a more carefully designed retrieval layer, but the goal is the same: reliable access to the information the workflow actually needs.

Skills plus connectors

Skills define the reusable steps. Connectors provide access to the applications and data. Together, they let Claude follow your team’s process while working with the systems your team already runs.
