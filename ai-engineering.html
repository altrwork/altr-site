What our AI development work covers.

Four kinds of build. Most projects use more than one.

  • Custom AI agents

    An agent reads what comes in, decides what to do next, and works across your tools, with a person approving anything that matters. Orders, bills, intake and reports are the usual starting points. Our page on AI agent development covers the kinds of work agents take off a team.

  • MCP connectors

    A connector is an MCP server: the piece that lets Claude reach your records and answer a real question about your buildings, orders or contracts instead of guessing. We deploy ours on Cloudflare Workers. People add the connector in Claude and sign in through a browser, so there is no API key to paste into a file.

  • Internal tools

    A process held together by spreadsheets and memory becomes a tool the team can run. For product teams, that can mean coding agents inside the repo: automated code review, security review agents and MCP tools that give the agent the context it needs.

  • Customer-facing AI

    AI your customers touch directly: an upload that turns a photo into a production brief, or a phone agent that runs a first conversation and hands a written summary to a person. The customer gets a faster answer, and a person still signs off on the result.

How an engagement runs.

  • Scope

    We start with the problem, the users and what good looks like. If something is worth building, you get a scope and a price in writing before anything starts.

  • Build

    We build around your existing stack, permissions and approval paths. Connecting to your tools is usually a connector, not a migration.

  • Control

    Permissions, human review, fallback behavior, logs and ownership are defined before the system goes live. Anything that does not match your records waits for a person.

  • Hand over

    Your team keeps the prompts, system instructions, documentation and operating notes, so the people who own the workflow can keep changing it without us.

Not everything needs AI. If the steps never change and the information arrives clean, a plain automation is cheaper, and we will say so. If that sounds like your process, start with our workflow automation consultant in Tampa page.
