Claude in CRE: Scheduled Tasks

Jarred RobidouxAugust 13, 20263 min read

Commercial real estate has always been a data game. Scheduled Tasks make it possible to keep a pulse on capital and local markets without starting the same research from scratch.

The routine

Scheduled Tasks let Claude run automatically on a recurring basis or on demand. Describe the job once, connect the right data, and let the routine produce the next digest.

What are Scheduled Tasks?

Instead of starting each task from scratch—“pull all recent sales data from the county appraiser’s site”—you define the task once and choose when it should run.

The task can operate across devices. Once a run finishes, the output is available from your phone or computer, which makes recurring research useful even when you are away from the desk.

Create the task with Claude

From Claude’s home menu, open Scheduled and choose Create with Claude. Claude will ask questions about the goal, schedule, source data, output format, and any limits it should observe.

For a local-market tracker, the source can be a custom CRE connector that reads county appraiser and deed data. Select the connector, choose the recent-sales tool, specify the market and lookback window, and confirm the format of the digest.

Run it on demand or on a schedule

You can manually run a Scheduled Task whenever a question is urgent, or let it operate on a weekly or daily cadence. A recent-sales digest might surface every property sold in the market during the previous seven days, then organize the properties and supporting data for review.

The important change is that tracking becomes a routine rather than a prompt someone has to remember to write.

Other CRE routines

  • Track capital-markets updates.
  • Summarize the client pipeline and prepare follow-ups.
  • Monitor mortgage-loan maturities.
  • Watch local sales and ownership changes.
  • Prepare a recurring property-operations digest.

Put the four pieces together

Skills carry the process. Connectors reach the live systems and public data. Cowork handles the files. Scheduled Tasks make the work recur without someone sitting at the computer.

That is the path from “we use Claude” to a real operating workflow that surfaces important details to the team every day.

Working idea

If your team checks the same source on the same cadence, it is a candidate for a Scheduled Task.
