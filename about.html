About altr

We help small and medium-sized businesses do more with the team they have. We find the work that eats their week, automate it, and train their people to use AI on the rest.

Two founders, no hand-offs: the people on your first call are the people doing the work. Based in Tampa, in person across Tampa Bay and remote everywhere else.

How we work.

  • The model is not the product.

    What makes AI useful is the context, permissions, review steps and hand-off around it.

  • Understand the work first.

    If nobody can say how a job runs today, automating it only makes the confusion faster.

  • Boundaries create trust.

    A good system knows what it can touch, when to ask for approval and when it does not know enough.

  • Your team should be stronger after.

    We leave the notes, prompts and workflows behind, so you never depend on us to keep it running.
