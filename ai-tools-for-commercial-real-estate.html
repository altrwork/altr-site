AI tools for commercial real estate, by the job they do

Start from the job, such as finding comps, abstracting a lease or drafting a BOV. Then pick the kind of tool and check it before you trust it.

Short answer

AI tools for commercial real estate fall into seven jobs: general assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT, data and comps platforms, lease abstraction, underwriting, CRE CRMs, public-record connectors, and document drafting. Most commercial real estate teams pair one general AI assistant with one or two specialist tools, and check each tool's data source and license terms before trusting it.

Where do general assistants like Claude and ChatGPT fit?

Everyone on the team touches them: reading an OM, emailing an owner, cleaning a rent roll, drafting a memo. Both create spreadsheets, documents and decks, and both connect to other systems over MCP.

Their limit is data. They know nothing about a specific parcel, lease or last quarter's comps until you give them the material or connect a source. See Claude vs ChatGPT for business and AI for commercial real estate brokers.

Can AI tools use CoStar data?

Not freely. CoStar's public terms of use restrict sharing credentials with AI agents, scraping, and using its data as input to generative AI tools outside CoStar. An assistant should not log in or read exported screens for you. The terms and the alternative are in can Claude connect to CoStar? Read the AI terms of every licensed source before you connect it.

What about lease abstraction and underwriting tools?

These are the most mature specialist categories: the documents repeat and the fields are well defined. Prophia reads lease PDFs into abstracts with each field linked to its clause. ARGUS Enterprise from Altus Group runs cash flow and valuation; Clik.ai pulls rent rolls and T12s into a model.

A general assistant handles a first pass on one lease or rent roll, especially with a Skill that holds your fields. Specialist tools earn their place at portfolio volume. More in rent roll and lease abstraction and AI underwriting.

How do public-record connectors fit?

A connector gives an assistant cited access to county parcels, owners, deeds and sales. It answers "who owns this and what did it last sell for" from the record, not a licensed screen.

Our county records connector covers Hillsborough, Pinellas, Pasco and Polk inside Claude, with ownership, sale history, comps and branded valuation documents. The same approach works wherever a county publishes its roll and sales as files; MCP server for commercial real estate covers the build. Public records are dated and rarely carry rents or leases. Florida property appraiser data shows what the roll holds.

Where does a CRE CRM like mortr fit?

Buildout combines CRM with marketing tools. Dealpath focuses on investment teams' deal pipelines. mortr, our own CRM, looks up county property appraiser records, manages contacts and deals, and generates broker opinions of value in your branding from dated public records.

How should a CRE team choose?

Pick one job the team repeats every week and the smallest tool that does it well.

  1. Name the job and who does it today.
  2. List the data it needs, and whether you own or license that data.
  3. Check each license for restrictions on AI use.
  4. Try a general assistant on the job first, with a Skill for your firm's steps.
  5. Add a specialist tool only where volume or consistency justifies it.
  6. Require every tool to show where a figure came from.

Common questions

What are the main AI tools for commercial real estate?

AI tools for commercial real estate cover seven jobs: general assistants, data and comps, lease abstraction, underwriting, CRMs, public-record connectors and document drafting. Most commercial real estate teams pair one general AI assistant with one or two specialist tools.

Do I need a specialist CRE AI tool or is a general assistant enough?

For single documents and drafting, a general assistant with a Skill for your firm's steps is often enough. Specialist tools earn their place at portfolio volume, where consistent fields, review workflows and integrations matter.

Can AI pull county property records?

Yes, through a connector built on the county's published data. It can answer ownership, parcel and sale questions with a citation to the record. Public records are dated and rarely include rents or lease terms.
