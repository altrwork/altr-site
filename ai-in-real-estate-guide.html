Free 18-page field guide

Move AI from the chat window into the way your real estate team works.

A free 18-page PDF for commercial real estate teams on reusable playbooks, connected systems, local file work and recurring routines. Built from our Claude in CRE series and written to apply whether your team uses Claude, ChatGPT or both.

No invented benchmarks. Four applied CRE workflows, an eight-question readiness scorecard, a 30-day pilot plan and a one-page workflow canvas.

A field guide your team can use in one sitting.

01

The four-layer operating model

See how playbooks, connections, workspace access, and routines turn a capable model into useful work.

02

Four applied CRE examples

An office-condo underwriting playbook, research on an 80,000 SF industrial building, an underwriting model built in place, and a weekly recent-sales digest, each from source to human review.

03

Use cases across the business

Find practical starting points for acquisitions, brokerage, asset management, operations, development, and capital markets.

04

A scorecard, a canvas and a pilot plan

Score a candidate workflow on eight yes-or-no questions, define its inputs, systems, output, reviewer and first safe scope on one page, then test it on real work over four weeks.

This is not a market survey.

We did not manufacture adoption statistics or pretend every team is at the same stage. This guide packages what we have learned from putting AI into real workflows: the systems that need to connect, the controls that need to exist, and the work that makes a strong first pilot.

Product details move faster than the method, so the web versions are kept current: Skills, Connectors, Cowork, which Anthropic began merging into everyday Claude in September 2026, and Scheduled tasks.
