The engagement

Three phases

A useful strategy makes the path smaller and clearer. It identifies the work that matters, tests the assumptions with the team, and gives technology a defined job.

  1. 01

    Map

    We sit with you and your team to understand how work gets done today, then decide how AI moves time, margin, and revenue.

  2. 02

    Build

    Our engineers build it, connect it to the systems you already use, and put it into production. Each build is priced up front and you approve it before it starts.

  3. 03

    Adoption

    We train the team that has to use it and stay until its part of their daily job.

After launch, most clients keep us on as their embedded engineering team.
