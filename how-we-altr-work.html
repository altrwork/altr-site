The engagement

Three decisions before a deployment.

A useful strategy makes the path smaller and clearer. It identifies the work that matters, tests the assumptions with the team, and gives technology a defined job.

  1. 01

    Map

    Document the trigger, sources, decisions, handoffs, exceptions, and reviewer behind a workflow your team already runs.

  2. 02

    Prove

    Run the improved process on real work, compare it with the current baseline, and make the guardrails visible.

  3. 03

    Sequence

    Choose what the team can adopt now, what needs a custom system, and what should stay out of the plan entirely.
