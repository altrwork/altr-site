What we build for CRE teams.

Much of a commercial real estate team's week is reading and retyping: ownership records, OMs, rent rolls, comps, CRM notes. We build systems that do that work on their own and bring your people only what does not add up, such as an assumption that is missing or a figure that does not match its source. Your people keep the judgment and the final call.

The work we have built for Tampa Bay teams is concrete. A county records connector puts Hillsborough, Pinellas, Pasco and Polk parcels, owners and recorded sales inside Claude, with every figure naming its source file and date. A branded BOV comes from a property address, in your firm's logo and colors. We set up underwriting and rent-roll and lease abstraction so inputs arrive organized and assumptions stay visible. Market monitoring watches recorded sales, ownership changes and debt signals on a schedule and flags the exceptions. mortr, our CRM for CRE teams, puts property lookup, contacts, deals and BOVs in one place.

Not every team needs a build. Many start with training and a few reusable Skills, and we say so when that is enough. There is no CoStar connector, and CoStar's terms bar AI use of its data, so we work from public records, your own files and systems that offer an authorized connection (Can Claude connect to CoStar?). We are based in Tampa and work with CRE firms across the US. For the complete model, download the AI in Real Estate field guide.

Questions CRE teams ask.

How can commercial real estate teams use AI?

Commercial real estate teams can use AI to organize property research, prepare underwriting inputs, work across approved files and systems, draft review-ready deliverables, and monitor recurring market signals. The routine work runs on its own, and your people get what does not add up. They keep the assumptions, investment decisions, client commitments, and licensed data.

What CRE workflow should a team automate first?

Start with a repeated workflow that has stable inputs, a clear output, and an identifiable reviewer. Property research, CRM updates, recurring market summaries, document intake, and first-pass diligence are usually better starting points than investment decisions or irregular negotiations.

Can AI connect to CoStar?

Not as of September 2026. There is no CoStar connector for Claude, and CoStar's public terms bar sharing passcodes with AI agents, scraping, and using its data as input to generative AI. AI can do the work around CoStar using public records, your own files, and systems with authorized connectors or APIs.

Does AI replace brokers, analysts, or asset managers?

No. A well-designed CRE workflow uses AI to collect inputs, follow repeatable steps, and prepare review-ready work. Brokers, analysts, asset managers, and principals remain responsible for assumptions, source validation, judgment, relationships, and final decisions.

How does altr start an AI project with a CRE team?

We start by mapping one real workflow: its sources, assumptions, handoffs, approvals, exceptions and final deliverable. We teach the team to run the improved process with AI, and build custom software only when the workflow needs system access, automation or deployment.

Is altr an AI consultant for commercial real estate firms outside Tampa?

Yes. altr is an AI consulting firm for commercial real estate teams, based in Tampa and working with firms across the US. Workshops and working sessions happen in person across Tampa Bay and remotely everywhere else. The Florida public-records tools cover Tampa-area counties; Skills, connectors and workflow design apply in any market.
