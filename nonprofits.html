Common questions

What non-profit teams ask first.

Do we need a large technology budget?

No. We start with one workflow and the tools you already own. A useful first step may be team enablement rather than custom software.

How do we protect sensitive information?

We define what the workflow may access, who can use it, where review is required, and which information should remain outside the system.

Will this replace staff?

The goal is to remove repeated administrative work, not the relationships and judgment your mission depends on. People remain responsible for decisions and communication.

Where should we start?

Choose a recurring process with clear inputs and a clear output: intake, event requests, expense reporting, or recurring funder research.

Bring us one process that keeps pulling people away from the mission.

We will help you determine whether it needs training, a custom build, or a simpler fix.
