What nonprofit teams ask about AI consulting.

Do we need a large technology budget?

No. We start with one workflow and the tools you already pay for. Anthropic offers discounted Claude Team and Enterprise plans to eligible nonprofits, verified through Goodstack, as of September 2026. A useful first step is often team enablement rather than custom software.

How do we protect donor and participant information?

We write down what the workflow may read, who can use it, where a person must review, and which records stay out entirely. In practice that means a business or team plan rather than personal accounts, no health or case notes in general-purpose chat, and outbound messages drafted for a person to send.

Will this replace staff?

The goal is to remove repeated administrative work, not the relationships and judgment your mission depends on. People remain responsible for decisions and communication.

Where should we start?

Choose a recurring process with clear inputs and a clear output: program intake, event requests, expense reporting, or a monthly funder report. If one person spends hours on it every week and can say what a good result looks like, it is a strong first candidate.

Do you work with nonprofits outside Tampa Bay?

Yes. We are based in Tampa and work in person across Tampa Bay. Nonprofits anywhere else in the US work with us remotely, with the working sessions over video and the same handover at the end.

Bring us one process that keeps pulling people away from the mission.

We will help you determine whether it needs training, a custom build, or a simpler fix.
