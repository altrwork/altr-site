We help nonprofit teams use AI in program, fundraising, event, reporting and administrative work, and build the repeated parts that should not keep moving by hand. We are based in Tampa and work with nonprofits anywhere: in person across Tampa Bay, remote everywhere else.

What we build for nonprofit teams.

The valuable work is relational: serving people, building trust, raising support. We build around it, on the document handling, follow-up and system-to-system work that pulls staff away. Program intake becomes organized records with next steps attached. Funder research becomes a repeatable comparison with requirements gathered and source-backed notes. Event requests move from intake to drafts, schedules and follow-up. Board, program and expense reports are pulled from the files and tools your team already uses.

For spARK Labs by ARK Invest, a nonprofit innovation hub in St. Petersburg, we turned expense and event requests into reusable workflows, then connected Claude to the team's intake, membership, access and email systems through one secure endpoint. The system handles what adds up and brings staff what does not.

We start with one workflow and the tools you already pay for. An audit finds where your hours go, training gives your team workflows it can reuse along with plain rules on donor and participant data, and custom agents cover the work that needs a build. What nonprofit teams ask.

Do we need a large technology budget?

No. We start with one workflow and the tools you already pay for. Anthropic offers discounted Claude Team and Enterprise plans to eligible nonprofits, verified through Goodstack, as of September 2026. A useful first step is often team enablement rather than custom software.

How do we protect donor and participant information?

We write down what the workflow may read, who can use it, what gets flagged to you, and which records stay out entirely. In practice that means a business or team plan rather than personal accounts, and no health or case notes in general-purpose chat.

Will this replace staff?

The goal is to remove repeated administrative work, not the relationships and judgment your mission depends on.

Where should we start?

Choose a recurring process with clear inputs and a clear output: program intake, event requests, expense reporting, or a monthly funder report. If one person spends hours on it every week and can say what a good result looks like, it is a strong first candidate.

Bring us one process that keeps pulling people away from the mission.

We will tell you whether it needs training, a custom build, or a simpler fix.
