Questions people ask about the numbers

Why is build pricing not listed?

A build costs what its scope costs, and scope is unknown until the workflow is mapped. Quoting a number before that means padding it or walking it back later. After the call you get one fixed fee against written scope.

What is the difference between the workshop and a build?

The workshop leaves your team prompts and workflows they run by hand, in tools they already have. A build is software engineering: multi-step agent orchestrations that act on their own, MCP servers wired into your systems, deployed and monitored. Nobody has to sit there and drive it.

Can I use retainer hours to get something built?

No, and that is deliberate. Ten hours a month is advisory time: reviewing what your team built, keeping a deployed system healthy, or steering a decision before it turns into rework. Real engineering gets scoped as a build so you get one number and we carry the risk of it running long.

What is included in a quote?

A quote covers the consulting time it names. No separate charges for calls, documentation, or travel within Tampa Bay.

Do you require a long-term contract?

No. Builds are discrete pieces of work with a defined end, and the retainer runs month to month if you want. The three and six month terms exist because they cost less, not because we require them.

What if we are smaller than this?

Start with the workshop. It usually reveals that the first useful step is smaller and cheaper than expected.

Bring one workflow and we will price it.

Tell us the process your team keeps doing by hand. You will get a straight answer on whether it needs training, a build, or nothing at all.
