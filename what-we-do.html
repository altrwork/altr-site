Questions people ask about the numbers

Why is build pricing not listed?

A build costs what its scope costs, and scope is unknown until the workflow is mapped. Quoting a number before that means padding it or walking it back later. After the call you get one fixed fee against written scope.

Why quote a build if I can just buy hours?

A build is a defined outcome, so it gets one number and we carry the risk of it taking longer. Retainer hours are for work with no defined end yet. Most teams start with a build and move to a retainer once there is something to keep improving.

What is included in a quote?

A quote covers the consulting time it names. No separate charges for calls, documentation, or travel within Tampa Bay.

Do you require a long-term contract?

No. Projects are discrete pieces of work with a defined end. The retainer is month to month, cancellable with 30 days notice.

What if we are smaller than this?

Start with the call, which costs nothing, or the workshop. Both tend to reveal that the first useful step is smaller and cheaper than expected.
