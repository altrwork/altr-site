Questions people ask about the numbers

Why is build pricing not listed?

A build costs what its scope costs, and scope is unknown until the workflow is mapped. Quoting a number before that means padding it or walking it back later. After the call you get one fixed fee against written scope.

What does all-inclusive mean?

The $185/hr rate covers standard AI and software consulting time. No separate charges for calls, documentation, or travel within Tampa Bay.

Do you require a long-term contract?

No. Every engagement is a discrete piece of work with a defined end. Support is month to month and cancellable.

What if we are smaller than this?

Start with the call, which costs nothing, or the workshop. Both tend to reveal that the first useful step is smaller and cheaper than expected.
