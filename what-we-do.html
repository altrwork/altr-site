Questions people ask

Why are there no prices on this page?

Because the first thing we sell is time in a room with your team, and what that costs depends on how far along you are, how many people are involved, and where you are. We would rather quote you on a call than publish a number we then explain away. Ask and you get it straight, in writing, before you commit to anything.

How long is a session?

Long enough to get through real work and short enough to hold a room. We set the length with you on the call, and teams split it however suits them: one afternoon, two mornings, or weekly blocks while the work is still fresh.

Working session or team workshop?

Either. Pick the working session when one process matters most and the people who own it can be in a room; you get a written map of that workflow. Pick the team workshop when the goal is getting a whole group off zero at once. Plenty of teams do the session first and the workshop after, because the workshop lands better on a mapped process, but that order is a recommendation rather than a rule.

Why is build pricing not listed?

A build costs what its scope costs, and scope is unknown until the workflow is mapped. Quoting a number before that means padding it or walking it back later. The session produces the map; the build gets quoted from it as one fixed fee against written scope.

What is the difference between enablement and a build?

Enablement leaves your team prompts and workflows they run by hand, in tools they already have. A build is software engineering: multi-step agent orchestrations that act on their own, MCP servers wired into your systems, deployed and monitored. Nobody has to sit there and drive it.

Can we start with the retainer?

No. Advisory hours only pay for themselves once we know how your work actually moves, and that is what enablement produces. Buying the retainer first means paying us to guess, which is why it is third on this page.

What can retainer hours not be used for?

Building, and running sessions. Ten hours a month is advisory time: reviewing what your team built, keeping a deployed system healthy, or steering a decision before it turns into rework. It is not a standing workshop allowance and not embedded engineering time. Another session, another workshop, or real engineering all get scoped and quoted on their own, so you get one number for each and we carry the risk of it running long.

What is included in a quote?

A quote covers the consulting time it names. No separate charges for calls, documentation, or travel within Tampa Bay.

Do you require a long-term contract?

No. Builds are discrete pieces of work with a defined end, and the retainer runs month to month if you want. Longer terms exist because they cost less, not because we require them.

What if we are smaller than this?

Start with the session. Small teams are the easiest case, not the hardest, because the person who owns the process is already in the room. It usually reveals that the first useful step is smaller and cheaper than expected.
