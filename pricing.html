Pricing

What this costs, before you ask.

Start with a free call. Where we publish a number it is a floor from our actual quotes, not an aspirational rate card. Builds are quoted as one fixed fee against written scope, with hours named per workstream, once the workflow is mapped.

Most teams start with the discovery call. It costs nothing, it produces a decision either way, and it is the only thing here we recommend to everyone.

01

Discovery callStart here

A working conversation about one process you keep doing by hand. We map what actually happens and tell you whether AI belongs in it at all. No charge, and no obligation to build anything.

  • The real path the work takes, including the steps that live in someone's head
  • Where time, rework, and waiting actually go
  • The control path any future system would need: permissions, review, fallback, ownership
  • A straight recommendation to enable, automate, or leave it alone

What we look at

Free no obligation You leave with a read on the workflow whether or not you hire us.
02

AI enablement workshop

A working session run against your own tools and your own repeated work. People leave with prompts and workflows they built in the room.

  • Pre-session collection of the work your team actually repeats
  • Full-day hands-on session, on-site across Tampa Bay or remote
  • The prompts, workflows, and operating notes stay with your team
  • A written read on which processes deserve a system rather than a prompt

What a session covers

from $3,500 per session Full day plus preparation. Scales with team size and number of workflows.
03

Automation pilot

One workflow built and running, narrow on purpose. Enough to prove the thing works against real cases before anyone commits to more.

  • Discovery, then a working automation or agent for a single workflow
  • A test set defined before launch: normal cases, edge cases, and the ones that should route to a human
  • Human review points and exception handling built in, not bolted on
  • Logs and a comparison against the current process

See what we build

Scoped after discovery Quoted as one fixed fee once we know the inputs, integrations, and review complexity. A pilot is deliberately the smallest version that proves the thing works.

  • No long-term contract; the engagement has a defined end

Billed by the vendor directly to you, typically $25–$250/month at pilot volume depending on document count, storage, and testing frequency. We would rather you own those accounts than pay us a markup on them.

Fixed fees, not open hours

The $185/hr rate is how we size work, not how we bill it. Once scope is written, you get a fixed number with hours named per workstream, so the price does not move because a build took longer than we thought.

Common questions

What people ask about the numbers.

If your situation does not fit any of the above, say so in the intro and we will tell you what it would actually take.

Ranges

Why is build pricing not listed?

Because a build costs what its scope costs, and scope is unknown until the workflow is mapped. Quoting a number before that means padding it or walking it back later. After the discovery call you get one fixed fee against written scope.

Rate

What does all-inclusive mean?

The $185/hr rate covers standard AI and software consulting time. No separate line items for calls, documentation, or travel within Tampa Bay.

Commitment

Do you require a long-term contract?

No. Every engagement is scoped as a discrete piece of work with a defined end. Support is month to month and cancellable, because the handoff is built to make us unnecessary.

Fit

What if we are smaller than this?

Then start with the discovery call, which costs nothing, or the workshop. Both tend to reveal that the first useful step is smaller and cheaper than expected.
