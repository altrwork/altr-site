Pricing

What this costs, before you ask.

Scope drives price, so most of these are floors and ranges. They are the numbers from our actual quotes, not aspirational rate cards. Once a workflow is mapped we quote a fixed fee against written scope, with hours named per workstream.

Most teams start with a workflow audit. It is bounded, it produces a decision either way, and it is the only engagement we recommend to everyone.

01

Workflow auditStart here

One repeated process mapped end to end before anything is built. The deliverable is a decision, not a proposal.

  • The real path the work takes, including the steps that live in someone's head
  • Where time, rework, and waiting actually go
  • The control path any future system needs: permissions, review, fallback, logging, ownership
  • A recommendation to enable, automate, or leave it alone, with the reasoning attached

How the audit works

from $2,500 fixed fee Bounded scope, one process. Priced off our $185/hr all-inclusive rate.
02

AI enablement workshop

A working session run against your own tools and your own repeated work. People leave with prompts and workflows they built in the room.

  • Pre-session collection of the work your team actually repeats
  • Full-day hands-on session, on-site across Tampa Bay or remote
  • The prompts, workflows, and operating notes stay with your team
  • A written read on which processes deserve a system rather than a prompt

What a session covers

from $4,500 per session Full day plus preparation. Scales with team size and number of workflows.
03

Automation pilot

One workflow built and running, narrow on purpose. Enough to prove the thing works against real cases before anyone commits to more.

  • Discovery, then a working automation or agent for a single workflow
  • A test set defined before launch: normal cases, edge cases, and the ones that should route to a human
  • Human review points and exception handling built in, not bolted on
  • Logs and a comparison against the current process

See what we build

$4,500–$8,500 fixed fee Our standard pilot range. Position in the range depends on document volume, integrations, and review complexity.
04

Phase build

A full workstream rather than a single workflow: architecture, security and compliance recommendations, the AI components, the workflow design, and the instrumentation to evaluate it.

  • Quoted as a fixed fee against written scope, with hours named per workstream
  • Architecture, permissions, audit trails, and data retention addressed explicitly
  • Built alongside your team or your developer, with the boundary between scopes written down
  • Ends in transfer: prompts, documentation, and operating notes

Read the work

from $15,000 fixed fee Our most recent phase build was 80 hours at a fixed $15,000, split across five named workstreams.
05

Embedded engineering

An engineer inside your operating context on a recurring basis. For teams where the work spans groups and nobody can yet explain the whole process.

  • Scoped monthly, typically 40 to 80 hours depending on the commitment
  • In your meetings, learning the domain, shipping alongside your team
  • Agent infrastructure, review paths, and permissions built as they are needed
  • No long-term contract; the engagement has a defined end

How embedding works

$185 / hr all-inclusive Roughly $7,400 to $14,800 per month at 40 to 80 hours. Quoted as a monthly fixed fee once scope is set.
06

Ongoing support

Optional, month to month, and genuinely optional. The handoff is designed so your team can maintain the system without us.

  • Monitoring, iteration, and changes as the workflow changes
  • New cases added to the test set as they appear
  • Cancel anytime; no minimum term
$750–$2,500 per month Depends on system count and how often the underlying workflow changes.

Model and infrastructure costs are yours

Billed by the vendor directly to you, typically $25–$250/month at pilot volume depending on document count, storage, and testing frequency. We would rather you own those accounts than pay us a markup on them.

Fixed fees, not open hours

The $185/hr rate is how we size work, not how we bill it. Once scope is written, you get a fixed number with hours named per workstream, so the price does not move because a build took longer than we thought.

An honest no is a real outcome

If the audit shows the process is not ready, the inputs are inconsistent, or the volume does not justify maintenance, we say so and stop. An audit that prevents a $15,000 build has paid for itself.

Common questions

What people ask about the numbers.

If your situation does not fit any of the above, say so in the intro and we will tell you what it would actually take.

Ranges

Why are prices ranges instead of fixed?

Because scope drives cost and scope is unknown until the workflow is mapped. Once it is mapped, we quote a fixed fee against a written scope rather than billing open-ended hours.

Rate

What does all-inclusive mean?

The $185/hr rate covers standard AI and software consulting time. No separate line items for calls, documentation, or travel within Tampa Bay.

Commitment

Do you require a long-term contract?

No. Every engagement is scoped as a discrete piece of work with a defined end. Support is month to month and cancellable, because the handoff is built to make us unnecessary.

Fit

What if we are smaller than this?

Then start with the workshop or the audit rather than a build. Both are bounded, and both tend to reveal that the first useful step is smaller and cheaper than expected.

Bring one workflow and we will price it.

Tell us the process your team keeps doing by hand. You will get a straight answer on whether it needs enablement, a pilot, a build, or nothing at all.
