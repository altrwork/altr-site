Free Claude workshops for Tampa Bay small businesses

Bring one real task. Leave with a workflow you can reuse.

altr is one of the Approved Claude SMB Trainers, the organizations Anthropic and Tenex trained to run Claude workshops in their own communities. These hands-on sessions are for owners and operators who want Claude working on the tasks already moving through their business.
