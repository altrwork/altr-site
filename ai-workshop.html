Claude workshops for Tampa Bay small businesses

altr is one of the Approved Claude SMB Trainers, the organizations Anthropic and Tenex trained to run Claude workshops in their own communities. These hands-on sessions help owners and operators use Claude on the work already moving through their business. Bring a real task. Leave with practical prompts, a repeatable workflow, and a clearer sense of where AI belongs.

Who it is for

Small business owners and operators across professional services, home services, healthcare, and other local industries.

What to bring

A laptop, access to your Claude account, and one recurring business task you would like to improve.

Build something useful during the session.

This is not a feature tour. Bring the work that repeats and we will work through it in the room. You leave with prompts and operating notes written around your task, not someone else's demo.

Customer and project work

Draft follow-ups, organize intake, turn notes into next steps, or create a better first pass on proposals and updates.

Documents and operations

Summarize long material, compare documents, shape repeat reports, or turn a process into instructions the team can follow.

Safe, useful judgment

Learn what to share, what to verify, where human review matters, and when Claude is the wrong tool for the job.
