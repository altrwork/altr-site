The premise

Build something useful during the session.

This is not a feature tour. We start with one process you already run, the files or messages it depends on, and the person responsible for the result. The goal is a useful first workflow you understand well enough to keep improving.

What a session is

Use Claude on work your business already knows.

Bring the work that repeats and we will work through it in the room. You leave with prompts and operating notes written around your task, not someone else’s demo.

Customer and project work

Draft follow-ups, organize intake, turn notes into next steps, or create a better first pass on proposals and updates.

Documents and operations

Summarize long material, compare documents, shape repeat reports, or turn a process into instructions the team can follow.

Safe, useful judgment

Learn what to share, what to verify, where human review matters, and when Claude is the wrong tool for the job.
