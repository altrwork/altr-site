The premise

A Claude workshop on your work, not a product tour.

Buying Claude is not adoption. These sessions are Claude training for small businesses in the sense that matters: one process you already run, files you already have, and a person who owns the output. It is still a chat window until the work has a cadence.

What a session is

Learn how Claude can help the business you already run.

Bring the work that repeats. We work through it in the room. You leave with prompts and workflows that fit how you actually describe the job, not notes about someone else’s demo.

Your work, not a dataset

The session is built from the emails, documents, reports, and requests a small business already handles. Not a generic feature tour.

Judgment in the room

When to trust Claude’s output, when to edit, and when to leave the task alone. That skill lasts longer than a product walkthrough.

Keep what you build

The prompts and operating notes stay with you. Nothing about the session depends on us being in the room again.

Not the same product

Paid team workshops are separate.

Altr’s enablement and team workshops are paid engagements, quoted on a call. They are for a specific team, on that team’s process. These five public sessions are a separate product. Do not treat a quoted workshop as free.
