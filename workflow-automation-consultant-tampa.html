Discovery

We map one repeated process end to end: inputs, decisions, tools, handoffs, review points, and failure modes. This is where a process automation consultant in Tampa should start, and it is bounded on purpose.

Decision

Enable, automate, or leave it alone. Not every bottleneck wants a zap. Some need a clearer handoff, a named owner, or a prompt the team can run by hand. We will say so before anyone builds.

Enablement

A working session with your team, using your tools and your repeated work. People leave with prompts and workflows they built during the session rather than notes about someone else's use case. Enablement comes before any custom agent.

Deployment

Automations, agents, and connectors built around your existing stack, permissions, and approval paths. Connecting to your tools is usually an MCP server or connector, not a migration. Your team keeps the prompts, documentation, and operating notes.
