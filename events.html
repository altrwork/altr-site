Three formats

Enablement sessions, community demos, and workflow reviews.

Enablement session

Run on a team's own tools and repeated work, either hands-on with the few people who own a process or broader across the whole team. People leave with prompts and workflows they built during the session, not notes about someone else's example. Both formats

Community demo

A live build in front of a room. We take a real workflow, show what it takes to make an AI system reliable inside it, and answer the questions that come up when something breaks on screen.

Workflow review

A focused look at one process a team wants to improve, ending in a clear read on whether it needs enablement, a workflow audit, or an automation.
