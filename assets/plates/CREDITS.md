# Plate credits

Every plate on the site comes from the **Historic American Engineering Record**
and the Historic American Buildings Survey, held by the Library of Congress.
These are works of the United States Government and carry no copyright.

One country, one collection, one medium, and the subject is working machinery
rather than antiquity: engine rooms, turbine halls, generator floors. Each is
cropped off its film rebate, toned deep ink through copper to a capped highlight
around #D4C1A8 so it reads as lit metal on the dark ground, and cut to 1600x1000.

## Tone
Deep ink (12,10,8) through copper (150,80,40) in the midtones to a capped
highlight (212,193,168), with autocontrast at a 1.5% cutoff and a 1.15
contrast lift. The cap matters: a photographic highlight at full white is a
lamp on a dark page.

## What was removed
The Piranesi Roman views and the Diderot Encyclopedie trade plates were
replaced wholesale. They were two different countries, two centuries apart,
and their subject was antiquity rather than the work altr actually does.
The HAER measured drawings were considered and rejected: they run 56-76%
blank paper against the 3-11% of these photographs, so they render as empty
rectangles behind a headline.
