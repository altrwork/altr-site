Questions about mortr.

What is mortr?

mortr is a CRM for commercial real estate teams. It combines property record lookup, contacts, a deal pipeline, and broker opinion of value (BOV) generation in your firm’s branding.

Which counties does mortr cover?

Pinellas, Hillsborough, and Pasco counties, Florida.

Are the property records live?

Not minute by minute. mortr uses dated public records: parcel details come from the September 2026 county files, and sales results show the date of the file they came from. Deeds take weeks to be recorded, so the most recent month of sales always reads low. Check the county record before you rely on a figure in a BOV.

Does mortr show loan information?

For properties matched to securitized loan records, yes: balances and maturity dates appear alongside your research. Loan details come from dated servicer reports and may not include all debt on a property.

Can I use mortr inside Claude?

Yes. The same property records and CRM can be added to Claude as a connector. You sign in with your mortr account in a browser, with no API key to paste, and contacts, deals and documents saved from Claude appear in mortr.
