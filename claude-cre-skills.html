Claude in CRE: Skills

Jarred RobidouxAugust 10, 20265 min read

CRE teams are adopting AI quickly, but most are still using Claude as a first-pass underwriter or OM reviewer. Skills are how teams turn their own process into repeatable work.

The gap

According to research from First American Data & Analytics and DealGround, 66% of CRE professionals use AI weekly or daily, but only 5% trust it enough to inform real decisions.

Why usage is not the same as impact

There is a reason for that gap. Most teams do not know about Skills, so their use of Claude starts over with every conversation. Context gets retyped, steps get paraphrased, and the output changes depending on the prompt.

altr helps businesses get real value out of AI in two ways: enablement—hands-on work with the team to understand and improve current processes—and deployment—custom agent engineering built around the bottlenecks in the business.

Understanding Skills

A Skill is a reusable set of steps Claude takes while completing a task.

Every useful prompt carries two variables: context, the information Claude needs to understand the task; and steps, the process Claude should follow to complete it. Skills package both into a simple markdown file that Claude can trigger whenever the task appears.

That means an underwriting process does not have to depend on someone remembering the perfect prompt. The assumptions, checks, sequence, and delivery format can travel together.

Creating a Skill

Claude gives you three ways to start. Create with Claude lets you describe the job and have Claude draft the Skill. Write skill instructions lets you author the file manually. Upload a Skill lets you use a package someone else created.

For most teams, Create with Claude is the easiest starting point. Describe the underwriting workflow, answer the follow-up questions, then test the resulting Skill against a real deal.

An underwriting example

A reusable office-condo underwriting Skill might instruct Claude to:

  1. Read the offering memorandum.
  2. Identify what the OM does not cover.
  3. Confirm missing assumptions.
  4. Build the ten-year cash flow.
  5. Deliver the model in the team’s required format.

The value is not the checklist alone. The Skill can be tuned to your OM format, due-diligence checklist, investment criteria, and the way your team actually underwrites.

Next: give the Skill access

A Skill makes the steps reusable. The next part is connecting Claude to the applications and data your team already uses—RentManager, Yardi, AppFolio, CoStar, Argus, your CRM, and the deal tracker—so those steps can operate on live work.

Working idea

A Skill is the difference between asking Claude to improvise an underwriting process and giving it the playbook your team already trusts.
