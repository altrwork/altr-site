What an AI consultant in Tampa builds.

Most Tampa Bay teams we meet already pay for ChatGPT, Copilot or Claude, and the work still moves by hand. Someone opens a vendor bill, reads it, checks it against a job and types it into QuickBooks. Someone else emails a sub for a W-9, then emails again when the insurance certificate expires. We build the systems that do that work: bills matched to the right job and purchase order (follow one bill through), W-9s and certificates requested, checked and re-requested before they expire, calls taken and written up, and weekly reports drafted from the systems where the numbers already live.

Each one runs in the tools you already use, inside limits you set, and works on its own. Anything that does not match your records comes to you with a note on what looks wrong, and every step is logged. Your team keeps the prompts and documentation. Connecting to your tools is usually a connector, not a migration; see AI agent development.

We work with operations-heavy teams where a named person still carries repeated work and someone can own the system afterward: commercial real estate, nonprofits and ecommerce. A chatbot for your website is not what we do, and we will say so on the first call. If the steps never change and the information arrives clean, a plain automation is cheaper, and that is the work of a workflow automation consultant in Tampa. Some teams start smaller, with a workflow audit or team training.

We are based in Tampa and work on site in St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Wesley Chapel, Lakeland and Orlando, because mapping a workflow goes fastest in the room. Remote work runs the same way over video. Our free Claude workshops for small business owners are held in person in St. Petersburg.

What Tampa Bay teams ask first.

Is altr an AI consulting firm in Tampa Bay?

Yes. altr is an AI consulting firm based in Tampa, Florida, founded by Alex Britton and Jarred Robidoux. We work on site across Tampa Bay and remotely everywhere else. We also run team workshops and free Claude workshops for small business owners.

What does an AI consultant in Tampa actually do for a growing team?

An AI consultant in Tampa makes AI reliable inside work you already own. We map one repeated workflow, find where the time actually goes, then either teach your team to handle it or build the part that should not be redone by hand every week.

How is this different from hiring an agency to build a chatbot?

A chatbot is one interface. We build a workflow: defined permissions, limits you set, fallback behavior, logging and an owner. Your team keeps the prompts and documentation, so you do not depend on us to change it.

Where should a Tampa Bay team start with AI?

A call about one process. It produces a decision either way: training, a build, or leaving the process alone. Scope is agreed in writing before work starts.

What should I bring to the first call?

One process your team still does by hand every week: the inputs, the tools, who reviews the output, and where it breaks. Screenshots or sample files are enough.
