Start with the work.

We learn how your team works and find where AI helps and where it does not. A new tool does not change a business, so we redesign the work with the people who do it and help them put it into practice.

Most clients start with one of two things, and either stands on its own. An audit maps one repeated process: the documents, the tools, the hand-offs and who checks the result. You get a written recommendation to train the team, build something, or leave the process alone (how our strategy work runs). Training is hands-on, on your team's real work, for the people who own a process or as a workshop. People leave with the prompts and workflows they built.

Our clients are operations-heavy teams where a named person still carries repeated work by hand and someone can own the new way of working. That includes commercial real estate, non-profits and ecommerce. If you want a chatbot wrapper for your website, or a strategy deck with no owner, we will say so on the first call.

We are based in Tampa, work with teams across the US, and come to you around Tampa Bay (AI consulting in Tampa). If the process only needs a plain automation, see our workflow automation consultant in Tampa page. When the work needs a build, AI Engineering carries the plan into an internal system or customer-facing product.

Questions about AI consulting.

  • What does an AI consulting company do for a small business?

    It finds where AI fits in the work you already do, changes the process around it, and trains the people who do it. At altr that starts with one repeated process and ends with a recommendation: train the team, build a system, or leave the process alone.

  • How is altr different from other AI consulting companies?

    We start with the work, not a tool or a slide deck. Your team keeps the prompts, workflows and documentation. When something needs a build, our engineers carry the plan into an internal system or customer-facing product.

  • Do we need technical staff to work with you?

    No. The training is about judgment: when to trust the output, when to edit it and when to escalate. People work on their own tasks in the tools they already use.

  • How much does AI consulting cost?

    Fees are quoted on a call rather than published. Scope and price are agreed in writing before any work starts.
