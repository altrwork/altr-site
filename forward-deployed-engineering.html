The useful version of AI lives inside the workflow.

The teams getting value from AI are not just buying tools. They are redesigning repeated work so people, agents, and software can share the load.

That usually requires more than a demo. It takes someone close enough to understand how work actually moves, what needs review, where data lives, and where the team will resist a new process.

Altr embeds for that part. We build with the people who will use the system, then leave behind workflows, agents, and documentation the team can keep improving.

How we partner

Embedded first, then build.

Forward deployed engineering means the engineer works inside your operating context before designing anything. In practice that phase is enablement, and its output is a map of how the work actually moves, not a proposal. The build gets scoped from that map and quoted as one fixed fee. An advisory retainer is available afterward, once there is something real to advise on. What you are never buying is a seat filled by one of us five days a week.

Embed and learn

We work directly with your team in sessions built around their real repeated work. Our engineers learn your domain and where the biggest leverage sits before anything gets designed.

Build agent infrastructure

We set up the harnesses and workflows that let agents operate effectively within your business, tuned to your stack and standards.

Accelerate production

With engineers and agents working in concert, we ship production systems at a pace your team hasn't experienced before. Real features, real infrastructure, real users.

Transfer and compound

Everything we build stays with you. The agent, the harnesses, the workflows, the knowledge. Your team is permanently faster after we leave than before we arrived.
